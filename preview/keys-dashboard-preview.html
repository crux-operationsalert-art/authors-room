AI keys, teams and quota

Preview. This is the dashboard layout with your 12 keys (last four characters only) and their proposed team assignments. Usage shows zero because nothing has run yet. The live version is the /keys page in the app, refreshing every 15 seconds.

Quota belongs to the Gemini project, so every key draws on the same limits. Keys give each team its own identity, so you can see who used what and revoke one key without stopping the rest.

When quota runs out, every key stops together. Waiting jobs resume after the daily reset. Adding a key from the same project won't help, because the quota is shared. What does help: author-facing work goes first (automatic), milestone reviews move to batch, strict mode off, or a higher paid tier in AI Studio.

Add a key

In the live app: paste a key from this app's one Gemini project, choose a team or standby, and press Add. The key goes straight to encrypted storage (Supabase Vault); only its last four characters are ever shown.
