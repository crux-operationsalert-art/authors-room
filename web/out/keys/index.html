AI keys, teams and quota

Quota belongs to the Gemini project, so every key below draws on the same limits. Keys give each team its own identity, so you can see who used what and revoke one key without stopping the rest. Refreshes every 15 seconds.

AI groups

One group per AI company, from one free account each. All keys in a group share one daily allowance, so a group works, rests and is replaced as a whole. When a group is used up it rests until its reset and the next group takes over.

Models — what each one did today

Each model has its own daily allowance in the main project. The team uses every one of them, and a model whose allowance is used up rests until Google's daily reset (1:30 pm IST) instead of being asked again.
